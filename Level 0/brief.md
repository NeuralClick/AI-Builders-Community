## 1. VIDEO TITLE

**AI: What It Is and What It Can Actually Do**

---

## 2. LEARNING OBJECTIVE

By the end of this video, learners will understand the difference between **AI and AI models**, what modern AI can realistically do, what it is particularly good at, and where its limitations are. Most importantly, they should leave with a practical mental model of AI as something they can **use to process information, generate outputs and help solve problems**—rather than something mysterious or magical.

---

## 3. CORE MENTAL MODEL

**INPUT → AI MODEL → OUTPUT**

The model takes information in, uses patterns it has learned to process it, and produces an output.

The better the **input, instructions and context**, the more useful the output can be.
