### SCENE 3 — What can AI actually do?

**NARRATION:**

So what can you actually use AI for?

Quite a lot.

AI can help you **generate** things—like text, images, ideas and drafts.

It can **transform information**—for example, turning a long document into a summary, or rewriting something for a different audience.

It can **analyse information**—such as finding patterns, classifying content, or extracting useful details.

And it can **help you work with information**—answering questions, organising ideas, researching topics, or helping you think through a problem.

This is where AI becomes particularly interesting for builders.

Instead of thinking, "What AI tool should I use?"

Start thinking:

**"What task am I trying to improve?"**
