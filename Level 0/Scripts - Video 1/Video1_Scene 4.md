### SCENE 4 — What is AI good at?

**NARRATION:**

AI models are especially useful when a task involves **patterns, information and language**.

For example, imagine a business receives hundreds of customer enquiries.

An AI system could help read those messages, identify what each person is asking about, summarise the enquiry, and suggest an appropriate response.

Or imagine you have a hundred documents and need to find specific information inside them.

AI can help process that information much faster than doing everything manually.

The important point is that AI doesn't have to completely replace a person.

Often, the most useful role is to handle the repetitive or time-consuming parts, while the human remains responsible for the important decisions.

**VISUAL:**

A stack of customer messages enters an AI model.

The output becomes:

**Understand → Categorise → Summarise → Suggest response**

A human reviews the final response.

**ANIMATION:**

Messages rapidly flow through the process while the human reviews one final card.

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:**

**AI handles the repetitive work.
Humans stay involved where judgment matters.**
