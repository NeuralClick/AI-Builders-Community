### SCENE 1 — What actually is AI?

**NARRATION:**

You've probably used AI already.

You might have asked ChatGPT a question, generated an image, summarised a document, or asked AI to write something for you.

But what actually is AI?

At its simplest, **AI is technology that allows computers to perform tasks that normally require some form of human intelligence**.

Things like understanding language, recognising patterns, generating content, making predictions, or helping make decisions.

You don't need to understand the mathematics behind it to start using it.

What matters is understanding what AI can actually do—and where you still need to be involved.

**VISUAL:**

A simple collection of everyday tasks appears: a question, document, image, chart and email.

These flow toward a central box labelled **AI**.

**ANIMATION:**

Each task moves into the AI box, then a useful output comes back out.

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:**

**AI = Technology that can perform tasks requiring human-like intelligence**
