### SCENE 5 — What isn't AI good at?

**NARRATION:**

But there's an important catch.

AI can produce an answer that **sounds convincing without necessarily being correct**.

It can misunderstand an unclear request.

It can miss important context.

And it doesn't automatically know whether its answer makes sense in the real world.

So you shouldn't think of AI as an all-knowing expert.

Think of it as a very capable tool that can process information and generate useful outputs—but whose work may still need to be checked.

And this is one of the most important skills you'll develop as an AI Builder:

**Knowing what to give AI, what to ask it to do, and when to check the result.**

**VISUAL:**

An AI output appears with a large green check/question symbol.

Then three warning cards appear:

**Can be wrong**
**Can misunderstand**
**Needs context**

A human reviews the output before it is used.

**ANIMATION:**

The output initially looks complete, then the review step highlights potential problems.

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:**

**Useful ≠ Automatically Correct**
